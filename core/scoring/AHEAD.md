# AHEAD — Sit in front of retail, not next to them

Research framework. Not financial advice.

Dune is the **hole cards**. DexScreener / web are the **board**. THESIS translates both into English. That is still a *backward* read: who already bought, what already printed.

This layer is **forward**. Retail is a known algorithm. The edge is knowing what they will look at in the next 6–24 hours — and either sitting in that seat *before* they arrive, or folding because they *are* the bid.

You are not cheering a ticker. You are playing a table where the fish's next action is predictable.

## Two inputs, one seat

| Input | What it is | Poker analogue |
|-------|------------|----------------|
| Dune (INTEGRITY / RX / FLASH / COHORT) | Who is actually in the pot, whether the ladder is real | Hole cards |
| Public (DexScreener / web / X) | What the room can already see | Board cards |

A good hand with the board already out (listing headline, −99% candle, TikTok) is **out of position**. A quiet CLEAN ladder with no site yet is **in position**. Same cards, different seat, different play.

## Retail is level 0

Retail, as a mass, does this in order:

1. Buy the greenest 24h % / DexScreener trending print
2. Buy the headline (listing, KOL, TikTok clip)
3. Buy same-day FLASH that *looks* hot
4. Buy the copycat of yesterday's winner
5. Do **not** check net-buy wallet %, addon, second session, or whether the contract matches the ticker

That is **level 0**. They treat the board as the hand.

| Level | Thinks | Dies on |
|-------|--------|---------|
| **L0** retail | Green candle = buy | HOOPLA morning, CASHCAT listing hour, TOAD volume |
| **L1** naive fade | All volume is distribution | Misses CASHCAT Aug 3–5 (the real hand *before* the listing) |
| **L2** this scanner | Don't chase L0 fireworks; press future retail bid on *new* CLEAN names; **hold the book** | Farms that fake a quiet ladder, or hopping the book |
| **L3** farm | Fake T-2: FLASH + silence + 99% net-buy wallets (HOOPLA) | INTEGRITY ceilings |

If you only fade volume, you are L1. If you only chase FLASH, you are L0 with a terminal. L2 is: **be where retail will be, before they know the name — or be the one selling when they show up.**

## Information clock

Every Slack name gets a clock. This is *when the information is*, not how good the score is.

| Clock | Who already knows | Typical print | Seat |
|-------|-------------------|---------------|------|
| **T-2 PRIVATE** | On-chain only. No trending, thin or no story. | CLEAN ladder, SILENCE or early lore | **IN POSITION** |
| **T-1 SMART** | Winner wallets / second FLASH session. CT still thin. | COHORT or PRODUCT/EARLY + still net-green | **IN POSITION** |
| **T0 BOARD** | DexScreener trending, first FLASH_IGNITION print | Age ≤1d fireworks | **BLINDS** — look, do not TAKE |
| **T+1 CROWD** | CT / TikTok / KOLs. Retail *is* the bid. | WARNING, faded FLASH, farm pack | **OUT OF POSITION** |
| **T+2 EXIT** | Listing, CoinGecko, app headline on a *flip*. Retail thinks this is the start. | CLIMAX on a hop — not the book | **OUT OF POSITION** for *adds* |

**The book is different.** CASHCAT is FLAGSHIP (`book.json`). Aug 6 was a bad day to *chase*. It was not a day to sell the hold. A FLAGSHIP that is CLEAN stays **HOLD** at T-1. Do not clock the book as T+2 just because a listing already printed.

CASHCAT's *add* edge was T-2/T-1 on Aug 3–5. Aug 6 was a bad add. The position is still the book. HOOPLA opened at T0 and was T+1 by evening. FRONG on Aug 13 was a look, not a replacement for CASHCAT.

**TAKE requires IN POSITION (T-2 or T-1) plus THESIS TAKE rules.** T0/T+1/T+2 cannot be TAKE. That is how you stay ahead of retail instead of next to them.

## Slack voice (no codes)

Never write T-2, T+1, OOP, BLINDS, or "IN POSITION" in Slack. Those are for the engine.

Say it like a trader:

- T-2 → "Almost nobody is looking at this yet."
- T-1 → "The sharp money is already here; the crowd isn't."
- T0 → "This is the first loud print — wait."
- T+1 → "The crowd is the bid right now."
- T+2 → "The headline already happened. That's the end, not the start."
- FLAGSHIP / HOLD → "This is the book. A red day is not a sell. I don't hop."

## What retail does next

One sentence in the rundown. What will the crowd do in the next 6–24h, and do we want that bid behind us or in front of us?

| Clock | Retail next (shape) |
|-------|---------------------|
| T-2 | They have not seen this ticker. If it prints green on DexScreener, they will chase. We want that bid *later*, not now as the exit. |
| T-1 | Smart wallets are already here. Retail follows the leftovers in 12–24h. |
| T0 | They will ape the first FLASH candle this session. We wait for the second show or we fold. |
| T+1 | They are the bid right now. Someone is exiting into them. |
| T+2 | They think the listing/headline is the start. It is the end — **on a flip**. Not on the book. |
| FLAGSHIP | They will sell the only name that matters because a day went red, then hop. That is how they never keep a position. |

Fail the run if a PLAY has no **Seat** and no **Retail next**.

## How this sits on the stack

```
Dune hole cards     → INTEGRITY veto, then MES × RX × FLASH × COHORT
Public board        → THESIS decoder + DexScreener/web
Forward seat        → AHEAD clock + level-k + Retail next
Slack               → Thesis / Tape / Public / Seat / Retail next / Opponent / Kill
```

Opponent (THESIS) is *who is selling to whom now*.  
Retail next (AHEAD) is *who will be the sucker bid later*.  
You need both. Backward without forward is a recap. Forward without integrity is L0 with extra steps.
